# Failures that changed the research

**A useful negative result changes the next decision.** A failed gate that only generates another near-identical gate is not enough.

The programme repeatedly found that the difficult part was not producing more experiments. It was selecting comparisons that could reveal something about learning, interpreting costs correctly and tracing apparent improvements to their actual cause.

## Five lessons with practical consequences

**1. Perfect final scores can conceal the wrong question.** If ordinary repair solves every case, another format comparison may have no remaining error to explain. Qualify a consequential gap before adding a more elaborate learning layer. Do not manufacture errors merely to make the candidate look useful.

**2. Passing tests may miss the changed behavior.** In the paper's adaptation study, a later diagnostic found incorrect annotation propagation in a REL implementation despite passing the scored checks. The investigator's correction was preserved separately rather than credited as autonomous learner success. [Study S2](../package/PAPER.md).

**3. Extra information is not the same as a better procedure.** A factual handoff can match a sophisticated lesson system. Equal-information controls prevent the effect of possessing history from being attributed to the format or workflow around it. [Study S4](../package/PAPER.md).

**4. Average error can improve while important errors worsen.** The localization comparison retained the mean/tail trade-off rather than calling the lowest MSE universally best. Consumer priorities must be declared before choosing a winner. [Run119](../package/component-evidence/REPORT119.md).

**5. Research costs are nested.** Acquisition, failed attempts, feedback, checking and reuse can belong to different cost boundaries. Reusing a previously acquired method does not incur its historical model-call cost again, but an amortization claim must still account for acquisition. Unknown coordinator work, inference energy or total spend stays unknown.

## When a source is not yet an experiment

The independent range-only robot source did not qualify: geometry, timing, calibration and reference contracts remained unresolved. Finding a dataset is not enough to establish a valid benchmark. The public [source-review material](../package/source-review/) records that boundary. No held-out performance claim follows from the failed admission.

## A decision rule for the next run

Name the remaining error, the proposed mechanism and the result that would distinguish it from ordinary engineering. Preserve a fresh comparison when the method changes materially. If the simple approach covers the useful trade-off, keep it and move to a different justified question.

The point is not to celebrate a large count of failures. It is to retain useful code, retire exhausted additions and make the next decision better informed.
