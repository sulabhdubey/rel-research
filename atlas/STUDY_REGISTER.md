# Numbered study register: Runs 1–121

This is a **catalogue**, not a scoreboard. Numbered runs include source qualification, engineering repairs, interrupted attempts, retrospective analyses, planned work and experiments. They are not 121 independent trials, successes, inventions or equally informative studies. Unnumbered REL comparisons, literature reviews and the incident project have their own records.

For mapped entries we checked the local record identity and opening heading, not every source, result or historical attempt. Selected released reports and component summaries are linked below. A withheld record cannot be independently audited from this index alone. Runs 1–14 could not be unambiguously reconciled in the bounded numbered-record inventory; they are left as gaps rather than reconstructed from conversation.

The reviewed paper's S1–S6 labels are **study labels**, not a renumbering of this register. See [reproduction coverage](REPRODUCTION_AND_SCOPE.md) before interpreting a report link as a runnable replication.
