# Research record index: selected public evidence through Run121

This index describes the numbered research records covered by the atlas as of **5 November 2026**. Most entries identify a record and its topic without publishing the underlying evidence. The current coverage is:

| Access category | Number of runs | What is available here |
|---|---:|---|
| Selected public evidence | 15 | Links for 8 released reports, 6 component summaries and 1 source-admission review; each has its own evidence limits |
| Identified private record | 92 | A topic and record type; the underlying record is outside this public release |
| Unreconciled run number | 14 | Runs 1–14 could not be matched unambiguously to source records in the bounded inventory |
| **Total numbered entries** | **121** | **107 mapped records and 14 unresolved numbers** |

**This index is not a publication queue.** The private entries document the scope of the research history; they are not pending uploads. There is no commitment or date to publish the remaining records. Any later addition would need its own source, privacy, rights and evidence review.

Numbered runs include source qualification, engineering repairs, interrupted attempts, retrospective analyses, planned work and experiments. They are not 121 independent trials, successes, inventions or equally informative studies. Unnumbered REL comparisons, literature reviews and the incident project have their own records.

For the 107 mapped entries, we checked local record identity and opening heading, not every source, result or historical attempt. A private-record entry cannot be independently audited from this index alone. The 14 unreconciled entries are preserved as gaps rather than reconstructed from conversation.

The reviewed paper's S1–S6 labels are **study labels**, not a renumbering of this index. See [reproduction coverage](REPRODUCTION_AND_SCOPE.md) before interpreting a report link as a runnable replication.
