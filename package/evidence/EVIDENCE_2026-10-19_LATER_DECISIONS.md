# Later decisions under changed requirements

29 native worker attempts were recorded across a discovery step, an original loop and a corrected operational replication. The full acquired JSON Schema validator supplied real residual errors. The discovery learner wrote its own checking procedure, and later workers read and used it. This did not establish consistent complete repair or broad RSI effectiveness.

## Fair comparison and limits

All conditions received the same frozen REL, discovery code, factual feedback, current requirements, tools, model and model-phase budget. They differed only in the checking method: ordinary work, a fixed supplied method, or a learner-authored method. Two later task branches each received two repetitions per condition. This is one source library and one learner origin, not a long independent task sequence.

The discovery learner was not given the fixed procedure first. This is therefore not an evolved-versus-its-own-predecessor test. All arms had factual history, so the comparison also does not isolate retention versus no retention or REL versus no REL. Repetitions use separate native contexts. Corrected repetitions revisit exposed source tasks and do not provide independent confirmation.

The last ordinary object attempt hit the model-host deadline and was interrupted before submission. Its cases remain ungraded and its observed usage remains a lower bound; it was not replaced. On the three matched completed cases (omitting the second object repetition from all conditions), group passes are ordinary 250/262, fixed 255/262 and learner 255/262, including one learner timeout. No whole repair succeeds. This is a post hoc descriptive sensitivity, not a new independent test. The saved method does not win consistently.


Groups/instances are dependent. The primary endpoint is the entire selected contract, not a passing percentage chosen after seeing outcomes. Old requirements include two inherited failures; new losses of previously passing behavior and current-scope errors are reported separately in the [de-identified ledger](EVIDENCE_2026-10-19_LATER_DECISIONS.json). Both corrected ordinary feasibility tasks had a current-scope error before comparison admission.

The learner array workers repaired and asserted both known old failures, yet missed changed dynamic-reference behavior. One learner object worker ran a failing old probe and explicitly treated it as outside the stage; another repaired only one old defect. Ordinary workers also used factual history. These are concrete observations of reuse, partial applicability and incomplete transfer, not proof that a method name or retrieved lesson causes improvement.

## Engineering defects corrected

The original later loop inherited read-only flags from frozen source through metadata-preserving copies. Twelve of fourteen later workers encountered actual denied library edits. All original observations and costs remain, but their score differences are not a clean method-efficacy finding. Fresh byte-only working copies now pass actual authorized broker edits while frozen originals and protected inputs remain unchanged. Two file-type checks reproduce the old failure and verify the correction.

One original model turn completed but its outer job expired during grading. Its completed-turn usage was reconciled from actual event identities; the interrupted attempt was not relabelled completed. A post-budget unchanged-code diagnostic recovered the remaining checks. The corrected host persists model-phase identity, usage pointers and frozen output bindings before grading. Five real child-process cases checked success, exception, abrupt exit, hang and incomplete-phase rejection; corrected live runs exercised the normal path.

The optional [non-public source: host contract](https://github.com/sulabhdubey/recursive-evidence-loop/blob/507357e1d7a55335f9205134ad1c32ac4657b0bc/references/host-integration.md) now carries both conditional lessons. Main SKILL.md and the frozen study snapshot are unchanged. These are verified engineering clarifications; the comparison did not test a performance gain caused by the newly installed wording. Earlier instructions already required usable authority paths and evidence preservation, so the original faults also reflect execution failures.

A separate post-outcome source review found that a fixed-method worker removed older-dialect contains bounds. The [2019-09 specification](https://json-schema.org/draft/2019-09/draft-handrews-json-schema-validation-02#rfc.section.6.4.4) contradicts its assumption. Four diagnostic groups spanning both dialects scored baseline 4/4, frozen worker 2/4 and a coordinator-corrected copy 4/4. This correction is not learner improvement and does not change the original scores. The sole learner timeout also passed on one unchanged-batch diagnostic replay; its original unresolved outcome remains.

## Costs, sources and reproducibility

All 29 attempts recorded **at least 19,289,955 total tokens**, including the confounded loop, recovered completed-turn usage and an interrupted-turn lower bound; **17,279,488** were recorded cached input (a subset), and **2,010,467** were recorded uncached input plus output. Worker actions totaled **764**. Summed dispatch time was **17251.7 seconds**; concurrent time overlaps and must not be read as elapsed study time.

Parent/coordinator usage, complete engineering time, monetary cost and local/remote energy are unknown. Recorded qualification and diagnostic timings are separate. No token-per-complete-repair efficiency claim follows for a condition with zero complete repairs. The evidence therefore does not establish complete cost-effectiveness.

Sources: [2020-12 release notes](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/release-notes), [core specification](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/draft-bhutton-json-schema-01), [official test-suite commit](https://github.com/json-schema-org/JSON-Schema-Test-Suite/tree/55719286c33ae0d1823bfb65fea10338a19bc37c), and [SWE-EVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18470) as motivation, not a reproduced benchmark. jsonschema 4.17.3 was the editable baseline. A separately corrected 4.26.0 reference passed the complete selected groups after a preserved disagreement; this is not a claim of newly discovered upstream defects.

Raw traces, acquired source, exact admissions and the research host remain outside this private package. The summary is inspectable but not a standalone reproduction of the model experiment. No publication or external message was sent. Keep the learner method experimental. A future test needs explicit inherited-defect versus preserved-behavior obligations and new source tasks.
