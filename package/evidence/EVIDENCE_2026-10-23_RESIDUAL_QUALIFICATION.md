[CommonMark 0.31.2 service contract](https://spec.commonmark.org/0.31.2/). It was
an opt-in migration, not a newly discovered current upstream defect. Actual
unfinished ordinary-repair artifacts were replayed, rather than planting errors.

is permitted. Source inspection found ordinary parser changes, not answer lookups
or replacement with another library. These research patches are not a production
release or qualification of every plugin and API.

Every condition received the full specification and all 652 official paired
examples. Two fresh native gpt-6.1-sol/medium workers had 60 actions and 900 seconds
including startup, the same current REL core and the same bounded offline tools.
Both completed normally. Earlier workers used Luna and prose without paired
examples; the new results do not isolate the effects of documentation, model
choice or additional repair time.

The conditional design compared no episodes, raw historical evidence, and the same
evidence plus a short conditional guide. The guide was fixed before ordinary
execution. The primary 702 positions excluded exact history examples. Both ordinary
workers passed those too. Consequently all four history attempts were skipped.
No history-effect estimate or comparative efficiency result exists.
