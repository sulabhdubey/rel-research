# Lightweight observation retention — 24 October 2026

The deterministic compact-view candidate **did not meet the pinned scoped-adoption criteria**.
Across six paired cases, its acquisition-inclusive total-token change against
current REL with raw facts was **+9.5%**;
the later-only change was +15.8%.
These are observed descriptive differences, not a population effect estimate.
Final decisions tied: all 30 sessions passed all eight decisions. The candidate
reduced later tokens in only two of six REL pairs and failed the consistency,
total-token and uncached-token selection checks. It is **not promoted as the
default REL retention method**.

All conditions could read the identical complete archive for their case. Compact
conditions also received a deterministic table of every measured query with
source revision, exact parameters, route, work and result identity. Full result
values and timings remained in the archive. The table did not choose a route,
rank evidence by success or receive current-answer information. This is a field
projection with raw fallback, not semantic lossless compression.

Ordinary workers received the same compact view as an additional control. Their
acquisition-inclusive total-token change against ordinary raw handoff was
-2.4%. A representation benefit that also
works for ordinary agents must not be attributed uniquely to REL.
Ordinary compact handoff also increased acquisition-inclusive query work by
+6.2%, so its small token reduction was
a trade-off rather than a win on every measured cost.

The compact REL workers read 87,075 history-result bytes,
versus 115,380 for raw REL. They read the compact view in
all six tasks and also made 4 raw-archive read operations.
Thus smaller returned history did not imply lower complete task-token use.
This is descriptive accounting; it does not identify the entire cause of the
extra model effort.

Across the six acquisition archives, the pretty-printed JSON occupied
101,384 bytes and the field views
31,561. A post-run byte-only diagnostic found that
ordinary JSON minification retained every field in
55,381 bytes. No worker received
a minified-JSON control, so no task-cost benefit is claimed for that alternative.
The view's size advantage must not all be credited to semantic selection.

## Decisions, information and fairness

Six ordinary acquisition sessions supplied history. Each was followed by four
fresh workers: ordinary/raw, frozen REL/raw, frozen REL/compact and ordinary/compact.
No code, prior answer or learner-authored prose lesson transferred. Current REL
was not burdened with an artificial reflection requirement: all four conditions
had receipt-only feedback. Same model, tools, current requirements and maximum
allowances; equal maximum allowance is not equal executed effort.
The audit verified 12 typed attachments of the frozen REL skill and no skill
attachment in the 18 ordinary sessions. All 12 displayed compact tables matched
their source projections, and paired archives matched byte-for-byte. Attachment
proves the supplied version, not perfect application of every instruction.

Cases D1/D2 preserved data and changed aggregation. D3/D4 changed two source
revisions and aggregation. D5/D6 reassigned job labels to other requests under
stable data, so labels alone could not establish valid reuse. Every query route
was checked against separately implemented full-row arithmetic. Initial and final
decisions, raw work and failures remain separate measurements.

The six cases are instances of one existing generator, not six independent
production projects. This fixed-history design isolates a representation mechanism;
it does not demonstrate an autonomous long-sequence improvement cycle or broad
harness portability. The coordinator could read the evaluator. No significance,
equivalence, universal RSI or novelty claim follows.

## Inclusive cost and selection

The six acquisitions consumed 628,469 total tokens,
102,901 uncached input plus output and
3,406,100 query-work units. They are counted once in actual study
usage: 4,020,739 tokens, of which
3,285,120 were cached input, and
735,619 uncached input plus output.
Each deployment alternative is separately charged the complete common acquisition;
those alternative costs must not be summed as if they were additional runs.
There were no model-based lesson-authoring calls.

Candidate acquisition-inclusive uncached-token change: +1.3%.
Candidate acquisition-inclusive query-work change: -3.8%.
Construction/storage timings and source/read byte counts are included in the
structured record. Complete engineering effort, parent-model tokens, money and
whole-system or remote-model energy remain unknown. Token totals include cached
input; they are not a bill. SQLite VM counts are work bounds, not joules. The local
queries execute quickly, so large work ratios do not establish large time savings.

Pinned engineering selection checks:

- at_least_four_paired_token_reductions: **fail**.
- charged_total_tokens_at_least_10_percent_lower: **fail**.
- no_extra_failed_decisions: **pass**.
- query_work_within_10_percent: **pass**.
- uncached_not_increased: **fail**.

This rule requires at least 10% acquisition-inclusive total-token reduction,
no extra failed decisions, no uncached-token increase, at most 10% extra query
work and token reductions in at least four of six pairs. It is an engineering
selection rule, not statistical proof of safety or superiority.

The executed conventional cache keys source, revision and the complete request,
including aggregation. On a miss it measures all three routes before selecting.
It passed 6/6 later tasks with
0 exact hits and
7,163,700 query-work units. Its researcher
construction cost is unknown. It is a conservative ordinary baseline, not a
novel algorithm or a claim that every useful transfer requires an exact match.

## REL disposition

The installed 20-file core stayed frozen. This study evaluates a host-provided
observation view; it does not test a rewritten core instruction. Update the
version-bound evidence and examples, preserving losses and mixed outcomes.
Any optional helper remains scoped to its source schema and tested conditions.
A generic mandatory instruction or universal memory mechanism is not established.
